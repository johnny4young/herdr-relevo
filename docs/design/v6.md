# Relevo v6: whose turn is it?

Every row in Reviews, Runs and Agents answers one question first: whose turn is it. This page holds the
grammar the views share and the decisions behind it. The components live in `mods/relevo/ui.js`.

## The row

`lane · body · edge`, left to right:

- **lane**, a 24 px column with one glyph: a filled disc for *needs me*, the spinner for *in flight*, a check for
  *ready*, a ring for *open*, a small dot for *old*, a warning sign for *stuck*.
- **body**: the kind glyph, what it is in semibold, and the one reason it is here in plain words.
- **edge**: its age and the one action.

Rows are separated by a hairline that starts past the lane. A row waiting on you is tinted (`blocked` at 7%).
Selection is a full accent outline over `selected`. The selected row opens in place with its extra lines; the
detail (review pane, a result, compare, accept several) opens only on purpose.

## Glyphs and charts

28 SVG files in `mods/relevo/icons/`, on a 12 px grid with a 1.5 stroke and square caps, tinted with
`text_color` like `spinner.svg`. Small charts: `seg` (one box per check), `diffRatio` (five squares),
`ageTicks` (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 1d), `legs` (who did what), `band` (usage with a pace tick), `tag`, `checkbox`.

## Colour, type and motion

- Theme tokens only. A tint is a token with an alpha suffix (`t.blocked + "12"`); a test fails on a hex colour
  in a view. Two palettes are data, not styling: account colours in `config.js`, and the folder marks the agents
  list shares with the space-folders mod (`relevo-agents/folder-colors.js`).
- Herdr supplies the faces (`ui_font_family`, `font_family`); the mods bundle no font and keep to 11 and 13 px.
- No new motion: the spinner is the only thing that moves, as before.

## Decisions

- **A waiting agent stays open.** Its question, the command or plan it asks about and its numbered answers are
  always in sight, because answering a permission without seeing it is unsafe. Its other actions wait for the
  selection like any row.
- **Selection does not need the keyboard.** A clicked row stays open until Esc or another selection; before, the
  highlight only showed while the list had focus.

- **Needs me is a disc, not a capsule.** The design drew a 6 px vertical capsule in the lane. At the leading edge
  of a row it reads as an accent strip, which Relevo does not use, so the lane uses an 8 px disc.
- **Folder marks keep the sidebar's colours.** space-folders paints folders from its own palette, so the agents
  list reads the same values from one file instead of following the theme.
